Beyond Immigration: Deportation Impacts on UCLA Asian American Students

Background

After Donald J. Trump's return to office in 2025, immigration policies expanded Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) activities across workplaces, businesses, neighborhoods, and other public spaces.

The consequences of immigration enforcement affect many immigrant communities, contributing to family separation, financial hardship, psychological distress, and fear that led many immigrant families to avoid work, school, healthcare, and other public spaces altogether.

Map's Direction

Although immigration enforcement is often portrayed as impacting only those who are directly detained, our project emphasizes that its consequences extend far beyond the individual, affecting the emotional well-being, financial stability, health, education, and daily lives of families, friends, and entire communities.

This mapping application visualizes the geographic distribution of lived experiences, mapping the human consequences of ICE enforcement and making visible the indirect impacts that are frequently absent from public discourse.
